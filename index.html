Welcome! I'm Resham Gholap, a highly motivated research associate with hands-on experience in molecular and cellular biology, specializing in immuno-oncology research. My work focuses on independently designing, executing, and optimizing in vitro and cell-based assays to advance pediatric cancer research.

With over 1 year of experience in biomedical research, I've developed expertise in analyzing and interpreting complex experimental datasets, troubleshooting protocols, and implementing process improvements to enhance reproducibility and data quality. I'm proficient in a wide range of techniques including ELISA, lentiviral transduction, mammalian cell culture, and data analysis using Python and GraphPadPrism.

I'm committed to scientific rigor, regulatory compliance, and continuous improvement in fast-paced academic and translational research environments. My goal is to contribute meaningfully to advancing treatment options for pediatric cancers through innovative research approaches.
